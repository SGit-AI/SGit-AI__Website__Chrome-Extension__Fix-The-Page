Fix the Page: my rules

Every fix you keep, every site this extension may touch without a click, and the buttons to take each one away. Nothing on this page leaves your browser.

Rules

No rules yet. Click the Fix the Page icon on a page, type a title and tick "Always on this site".

Sites with access

Each site here was allowed by you, one at a time, through Chrome's own prompt. The engine runs on every page of a listed site to apply your rules. Remove access and it stops at once.

No site has standing access. The extension only touches a tab when you click it there.
